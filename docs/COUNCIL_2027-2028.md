## Recommendation

### Thesis

Rhythmguard 4.0 is a small, stable, token-file-native scale ratchet that design-system teams put in CI on their own product code. Its findings never change within a major, and every new capability must come from a named external user.

### Why this and not the alternatives

D's discipline is the precondition for every other direction: no buyer, agent or fleet can trust a drift number while messages change in five releases and the publish pipeline is red. B supplies the one capability gap real usage shows (doctor-school hand-derives its scale from Style Dictionary output; a DTCG export silently falls back). A supplies the one outreach test that costs nothing. A's `fleet` (nobody asked), B's full resolver programme (too large for one person) and C's agent bet (its own premise weakens as models improve) stay as options with a concrete trigger: build when a named team asks, on the record.

### User and buyer

The user is a design engineer on a 1 to 6 person design-system team whose scale lives in a token package (Style Dictionary or DTCG) and whose apps live in a monorepo or a few repos. doctor-school/ds-platform is the reference user. The buyer is the lead who approves a blocking CI gate; they pay in trust (stable findings, no-visual-change fixes, no telemetry), not money. No paid tier or hosted product through 2028.

1. Fix the companion OIDC publish (#61), add a release gate that fails while any package lags, add Node 24 and 26 to CI (#164).
2. Ship 3.11: #163 (`{ value, unit }`, `{group.token}` aliases, a warning when a token source yields zero spacing tokens), #161, #159. The high-severity bugs from the 2026-10-18 sweep (#298) belong here too.

4. Post the Primer config draft, confirmed by you before it goes out, and log it.
5. Send doctor-school one message that leads with their own `allowed-tokens.json` derivation and their 3.6.0 pin: what blocks an upgrade, and could a token source replace the glue code. Confirmed by you first. They were never contacted, so this does not break the no-follow-up rule.

### 4.0: the week Stylelint 18.0.0 ships, no later than 2027-02-28

- #166 `--preserve` ships first as 3.12 in December, the last 3.x feature.
- Node floor equal to Stylelint 18's; peer `^17 || ^18`; drop Stylelint 16 and Node 20.
- The promised report-moving defaults, once: `fixWith: "token"`, one default scale everywhere.
- Structured findings: an exported `findingKey()`; the audit reads structured data, not message text; baselines key on it.
- Contract tests: a snapshot of every message template that fails outside a major, and a `SURFACE.json` budget of rules, options, exports and presets.
- Deprecate, for removal in 5.0: the motion rules and config, presets beyond five, the `eslint-plugin-rhythmguard` alias (the real ESLint user uses the subpath).
- `docs/MIGRATING_TO_4.md`; test the upgrade on doctor-school's config and the 58 benchmark rows with zero false new findings.

### 2027

- **Q1.** Ship 4.0. Move the agent eval harness, outreach, Dev.to drafts and the State of Spacing generator to a lab repo. Publish the State of Spacing 2026-09 article once. Treat the 2027-03-31 checkpoint as a pace decision, not a kill.
- **Q2.** SARIF (#165). Read Style Dictionary output and DTCG with aliases directly, so a doctor-school-style team can delete its glue. Per-workspace scales (#174) only on request. Close #175 and #176 with written reopen conditions.
- **Q3.** One State of Spacing "consumer edition": consumers of five token packages measured against their scale, as the year's single outreach artifact. Open a Stylelint discussion about nearest-step suggestions on top of the v18 rules.
- **Q4.** At most one minor a month. Adopt Stylelint 19 within 14 days of release. Review the year; build `fleet` only if two named external teams have asked in issues.

### 2028

5.0: ESM-only, deprecated surfaces removed, at most 8 export subpaths, types from JSDoc, peer `^18 || ^19`. One State of Spacing edition. By 2028-06-30 choose: stay a maintained small tool; hand nearest-step checking upstream to Stylelint and shrink to the audit plus a config; or, only if three external teams run it across 5+ repos, build the fleet report as static HTML.

### Stop

New rule options, property groups, presets and scale research. Motion features. Less, MCP, and the @eslint/css port (#173) until @eslint/css reaches 25% of Stylelint's weekly downloads (5.8% today) or three users ask. New agent eval editions. Cold audit issues to OSS repos. More than one release a day. Message changes in patches. Tailwind class-name features. A Figma bridge, hosted dashboard or paid tier. Tracking stars and visitors.

## Member summaries

**A, market and adoption.** "Well built, almost no users, distribution plan unexecuted." Proposes drift measurement for DS teams as the code-side counterpart of Figma's Check designs: `fleet`, SARIF, design partners (Primer, PatternFly, NHS.UK, mittwald, Materialize), static dashboards, a funding decision only on indicators. Kill: fewer than two DS teams running fleet for eight weeks by 2027-06-30.

**B, design-systems practice.** Praises the ratchet and the "maintainer says where the truth is, tool learns to read it" loop. Faults the single project-level scale (modes, brands, density), thin DTCG support, JS theme scales, and outreach to the cleanest repos instead of consumers; "cleanliness" is not a number DS leads report upward, coverage by property is. Proposes the token-contract verifier: full DTCG 2025.10, `fleet`, Figma-variables diff from an exported file, JS theme objects.

**C, agent-native tooling.** Edition 1 cannot exclude a 20-point gap; the honest field claim is cheaper convergence, not better outcomes; stronger models drift less. Proposes the design contract for agents: `rhythmguard context` into AGENTS.md, `--format agent`, stable finding keys, tamper-evident baselines and suppressions, hook recipes, and a five-arm eval (rules only, contract in context, findings after, CLI plus hook, MCP) with 5 seeds to settle #176: ship MCP only if it beats CLI plus hook by 20% with an interval excluding zero.

**D, maintainer and architect.** Surface grew from 6.6k to 11.2k lines while adoption stayed flat; the plan did not survive agent velocity; releases and messages are unstable; Stylelint 18 absorbs "lengths must be tokens". Proposes the boring ratchet: freeze, 4.0 with every report-moving default at once and structured findings, message-template snapshot and surface-budget tests, deprecate motion and most presets, move outreach and evals to a lab repo, ESM-only 5.0. Kill: fewer than 3 dependents by 2027-09-30.
